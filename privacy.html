Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Who we are

This notice is issued by Fathom Signal, a Delaware corporation operating as Fathom ("Fathom", "we", "us"). It covers fathomsignal.com and the Fathom app (together, the "Service"). Fathom Signal is the controller of the personal information described here. Fathom is independent: no employer, agency or other organization where Fathom is used controls it or receives data from it. Questions go to team@fathomsignal.com, and a person will answer.

2. What we hold

  • Your email address. Used to send you one-time codes and notices. We do not use passwords.
  • Your team memberships. Which teams your email address belongs to, or, for an outside recipient, which reports are addressed to it.
  • Your nickname, which your team sees.
  • Your reputation score. One number per person, shared across all your teams.
  • Your device's notification address, if you turn notifications on. Every notification reads only "Notification".
  • What your team writes: topics, comments, Activity and reports, for the times listed in Section 7.
  • Applications, until reviewed.

We do not collect your real name, phone number, contacts, location or payment details. We keep no history of when or how often you use Fathom. The website sets no cookies for analytics or advertising.

3. How we use it

Only to run the Service, keep it secure, and meet legal obligations. We never sell your information, never share it for advertising, and never use it to train AI models.

4. How your identity is kept apart from what you say

  • No names on contributions. Our records of topics, comments, answers, proposals and votes carry no name, email address or account.
  • One member, one voice. While a topic, comment or vote is open, the system uses a sealed key for that item to count one answer or vote per member and to adjust scores. The key is destroyed when the item closes. After that, no one, including Fathom, can link the item to the person behind it.
  • What members see. Members see what is posted, the tallies and the team's Activity, and never who wrote, proposed, answered or voted.

5. What happens to what you contribute

  • AI processing. Topics, comments and answers are filtered, and reports written, by Google's Gemini models on Google Cloud Vertex AI. These run inside a Google Cloud environment set up to the U.S. government's FedRAMP High standard, in the United States. We switch off Google's caching and send Google the words only, never names or email addresses.
  • Voice. A recording is sent to Google for transcription only. It is never stored by Fathom.
  • Filtering. If something reads as an attack on a person, could identify you, or shares something sensitive that does not belong in the group, the filter tells you why and may suggest a revision. You decide what to send. Anything the filter judges malicious or harmful is held back from the report.
  • Answers. Answers are held, with no name attached, only until the topic's report is written, and are then deleted. If a topic closes without enough answers, its answers are deleted at once.
  • Reports. A report is written only when a topic reaches its minimum number of answers, which is never fewer than three. A report lasts one minute from each recipient's first opening and is deleted after seven days if never opened. Fathom keeps no copy once every recipient's copy is gone.

9. Legal process

We disclose information only when a valid legal order requires it. We will challenge overbroad demands and notify affected people where the law allows. An order can reach only what we hold at that moment, listed in Section 7. It cannot reach who wrote, proposed, answered or voted on anything once that item has closed, or any recording, because we do not keep them.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access follows least privilege. No system is perfectly secure, so we hold as little as possible for as short a time as possible. Security researchers can reach us through fathomsignal.com/.well-known/security.txt.
